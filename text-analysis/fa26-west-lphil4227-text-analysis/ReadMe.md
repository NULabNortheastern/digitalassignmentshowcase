## Summary of Module
This module introduces three digital resources from the Women Writers Project for exploring early women's writing in English: Women Writers Online (WWO), the Women Writers Vector Toolkit (WWVT), and Intertextual Networks. 

## Learning Goals
- Understand what the Women Writers Project is and how its digital collections are built from encoded transcriptions of early printed texts.
- Understand how different digital tools can support research on a collection, from close reading of a single text to patterns across a whole corpus.
- Think critically about digital archives: what gets saved, whose writing is missing, and how that shapes research results.

## Learning Objectives
- Search within a single text and across the WWO collection, and filter results by genre and date.
- Use the Women Writers Vector Toolkit to find words used in similar ways and to compare word usage across corpora.
- Navigate Intertextual Networks, starting from a source text, to see which works a writer quotes, cites, or references.
- Discuss archival silences and how gaps in what survived affect the conclusions we can draw.
